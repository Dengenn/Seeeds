Your rights

Wherever you live, you can use the rights below. They come from laws such as the UK and EU GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and other privacy laws. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Access

Get a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correction

Fix anything that's inaccurate or incomplete.

Deletion

Ask us to erase your data, subject to legal record-keeping.

Portability

Receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.

Objection and restriction

Ask us to stop or limit certain uses, such as profiling.

Withdraw consent

Turn off marketing emails or cookies at any time.

Opt out of sale or sharing

Stop advertising cookies that count as selling or sharing.

Non-discrimination

Using these rights will never change your prices or service.

Make a request

Tell us what you'd like us to do. It's free, and you can also make a request through an authorised agent.

Verification and timing

To protect you, we'll confirm your identity by emailing the address on your account before we act. We respond within one month (UK and EU) or 45 days (US). Where the law allows, complex requests may take longer, and we'll let you know if so.
