The mark

Drawn on the form of the shamrock emoji: three heart-shaped leaves meeting at the center, with the short curved stem below. No inner lines, no sparkle, no detail. In the hero version the face is satin gold and the outer edge carries a thin polished gold rim, so the whole mark reads as one cast gold piece, like a button or cord stopper. This is my own drawing of that shape, not a copy of any emoji artwork. The shamrock is strongly linked to Ireland, so confirm the trademark with an attorney before you commit.

The wordmark

Dribbble's wordmark is bold, lowercase, friendly retro lettering where the letters flow into each other. That is the feeling here. These are free script fonts in the same spirit (not Dribbble's own font), converted to clean outlines so they stay identical everywhere. Option A is my pick: its tall d gives the word a lift.

A. PacificoBold, rounded and flowing. The closest to the Dribbble feel.
B. Oleo ScriptHeavier and more retro, strong on small labels and tags.
C. PlayballSlimmer and more refined, closest to a fashion-house signature.

Colorways

Gold is now the lead color, and green is gone from the system entirely. On dark grounds the mark is cast gold. On light grounds it is black enamel in a gold rim. Ivory is the soft alternative on black. Foil is the single-ink version, with no rim, for stamping and embroidery.
