2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, password, phone number.
  • Order details: delivery and billing address, items purchased, sizes, order history and returns.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Messages: anything you send us through customer service, email sign-up or privacy requests.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, products clicked and approximate location (country or city).
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Cookies below.

3. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders, handle returns and provide customer support.
  • To run your account, harvest (cart) and favourites.
  • To send order updates and, if you opt in, news about drops and the stories behind them.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud, keep the site secure and meet our legal obligations.

4. Legal bases (UK and EU visitors)

  • Contract: to fulfil your orders and run your account.
  • Consent: for marketing emails and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to improve our site, prevent fraud and keep our services secure.
  • Legal obligation: for tax, accounting and consumer-law records.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run seeeds, under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Payment processors and fraud-prevention services.
  • Delivery and logistics partners.
  • Website hosting, email and customer-service tools.
  • Analytics and advertising partners, only if you allow those cookies.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some advertising cookies may count as "selling" or "sharing" under certain US state laws. You can opt out on the Your Privacy Rights page.

7. How long we keep it

We keep order and transaction records for as long as tax and consumer law require (typically 6 to 7 years). We keep account data while your account is open. Marketing preferences last until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for up to 26 months. When we no longer need information we delete or anonymise it.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and trusted payment providers to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Our kids' range is bought by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.
